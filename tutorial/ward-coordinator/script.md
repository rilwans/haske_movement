# Ward coordinator tutorial: sign in and onboard a canvasser

About 2 minutes of narration, voiced by Obinna (ElevenLabs). The audio is in `audio/`. Each section below is one clip with its own voice-over file.
The narration never says the default password out loud.

**Demo details (local only, all made up)**

| | |
|---|---|
| Ward coordinator | Amina Bello, Lanwa ward, 0800 555 0101 |
| Canvasser to add | Musa Ibrahim, 0800 555 0102 |
| New password to type | any 8 or more characters; it is hidden on screen |

Run `./reset-demo.sh`, then open the app, before each full take. It puts everything back to the Sign in screen.

Record a clip with `./record-clip.sh 02-sign-in`, and press Ctrl+C to stop.

---

## 01-welcome

**Voice-over:** 10.5 seconds

**On screen:** the Sign in screen, nothing typed yet.

> Welcome, ward coordinator. In this short video, you will sign in to the Haske app for the first time, choose your own password, and add a canvasser for a polling unit in your ward.

## 02-sign-in

**Voice-over:** 9.4 seconds

**On screen:** tap Phone number, type `0800 555 0101`. Tap Password, type the default password. Tap **Sign in**.

> Open the app. Enter your phone number, the one that was registered for you. Next, enter the password you were given. Then tap Sign in.

## 03-set-password

**Voice-over:** 16.6 seconds

**On screen:** the "Set your password" screen appears. Type a new password in both boxes. Tap **Save password**.

> The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose your own password. Type a new password with at least eight characters. Type it again in the second box to confirm it, then tap Save password. Keep this password private, and do not share it with anyone.

## 04-setup

**Voice-over:** 12.7 seconds

**On screen:** "Setting up this phone". When Android asks for location, tap **While using the app**. The home screen opens.

> The app now sets up your phone. You need internet for this, but only this once. If the app asks to use your location, allow it. When this is done, you will see your home screen, with your ward's name at the top.

## 05-open-form

**Voice-over:** 7.7 seconds

**On screen:** tap **Team** at the bottom. Tap **Onboard canvasser** at the bottom right.

> Now let us add a canvasser. At the bottom of the screen, tap Team. Then tap the Onboard canvasser button.

## 06-details

**Voice-over:** 13.3 seconds

**On screen:** tap each box in turn and type First name `Musa`, Last name `Ibrahim`, Phone number `0800 555 0102`. Leave WhatsApp and Email empty.

> Enter the canvasser's first name and last name. Then enter their phone number. Take care with this number, because it is what they will use to sign in. Only fill in the WhatsApp number if it is different. Email is optional.

## 07-polling-unit

**Voice-over:** 9.8 seconds

**On screen:** tap Polling unit. Tap `Lgea Sch Lanwa` in the list. If the search box has opened a keyboard over the list, the unit is still near the top.

> Next, tap Polling unit, and choose where this canvasser will work. You can search by name to find it quickly. You will only see the polling units in your ward.

## 08-save

**Voice-over:** 13.2 seconds

**On screen:** tap the tick on the keyboard to close it, so the **Save** button shows. Tap **Save**. The message "Musa saved" appears at the bottom of the screen.

> Check the details. If the keyboard is covering the Save button, close the keyboard. Then tap Save. You do not need internet for this. The canvasser is saved on your phone, and is sent as soon as you are back online.

## 09-check-status

**Voice-over:** 16.4 seconds

**On screen:** you are back on the Team tab. Tap **Added here**. With internet, Musa already has the green tick, because the app sends by itself.

> To check on them, tap Added here. When you see the green tick, they are ready to sign in. If you have no internet, you will see Waiting to send instead. Do not worry. It sends by itself when you are back online, or you can tap Sync now at the top of the screen.

## 10-handover

**Voice-over:** 16.1 seconds

**On screen:** hold on the green-tick row in Added here.

> Now tell your canvasser the phone number and the password shown here. The first time they sign in, they will choose their own password, just as you did. If anyone forgets their password, ask an Erims administrator to reset it. That is all. Thank you for building your team.
