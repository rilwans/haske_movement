# Candidate tutorial: following your campaign in the Haske app

Each section is one clip with its own voice-over file. The narration never says the default password out loud.

**Demo details (local only, all made up)**

| | |
|---|---|
| Candidate | Demo Candidate, 0800 555 0400 |
| Election | Demo State Governorship, 90 days away |
| Area | Demo State: 5 LGAs, 15 wards, 60 polling units |
| Figures | Invented voters and visits. No real campaign numbers appear. |

Run `./reset-demo.sh` before each full take. `./reset-demo.sh remove` takes the demo records back out.

## Part 1: Getting started

## 01-welcome

**On screen:** the Sign in screen.

> Welcome. This short video will show you how to follow your campaign using the Haske app. You do not need to be good with phones. You can pause the video at any time, and go back if you miss something.

## 02-what-it-does

**On screen:** the Sign in screen.

> The app gives you a clear picture of how your campaign is doing, across all the local governments in your election. You can see how many voters have been reached, how many support you, and where your team needs help. You only see totals. You will never see the names of individual voters, and nothing you do here can change the campaign's records. The figures in this video are made up, for training only.

## 03-sign-in

**On screen:** tap Phone number, type `0800 555 0400`. Tap Password, type the default password. Close the keyboard. Tap **Sign in**.

> Let us sign in. Tap the box that says Phone number, and type your phone number. Now tap the box that says Password, and type the password you were given. It will show as small dots, to keep it private. Then tap the green Sign in button.

## 04-set-password

**On screen:** "Set your password". Type a new password in both boxes. Tap **Save password**.

> The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose your own password. Tap the first box, and type a new password, with at least eight characters. Tap the second box, and type it again. Then tap Save password. Please keep this password private.

## 05-setup

**On screen:** the location question appears. Tap **While using the app**. The Overview opens.

> The app now gets your phone ready. Your phone may ask if the app can use your location. Tap, While using the app. You will then see your home screen, which is called Overview.

## Part 2: The Overview

## 06-headline

**On screen:** the top of Overview. Hold.

> At the very top is the name of your election and its date. Below that are the three numbers that matter most. How many days are left. What percentage of voters have been canvassed, which means visited by your team. And how many voters are ready to vote for you.

## 07-needs-attention

**On screen:** hold on Needs attention.

> Next is, Needs attention. This is the first place to look each day. It lists the local governments where something may be wrong. For example, no visits in the last seven days, no L.G.A. coordinator, or visits dropping quickly. If this box is empty, nothing stands out.

## 08-momentum

**On screen:** slide up to Momentum. Hold.

> Slide your finger up the screen to see more. Momentum compares the last seven days with the seven days before, so you can see if your team is speeding up or slowing down. Below that, the app tells you how much of the voters' list will be covered by election day, if your team keeps the same pace.

## 09-vote-count

**On screen:** slide up to Vote count. Hold.

> Next is the Vote count. It sorts the voters your team has spoken with into four groups. Ready to vote, are supporters who have their PVC and say they will vote. Supporters needing a PVC, still need their voter's card. Supporters unsure of voting, need encouragement. And Persuadable, are people who are undecided or leaning against you. They could still be won over.

## 10-ranked

**On screen:** slide up to LGAs ranked. Tap the sort button, choose **Most supporting**.

> Next, every local government is ranked. Each green bar shows how much of that area has been canvassed. To rank them differently, tap the words at the top right of this box, and make a choice. Here, we are choosing, Most supporting. You can also tap any local government to learn more about it.

## 11-team

**On screen:** slide up to Team strength. Hold.

> Team strength shows how many coordinators and canvassers you have in each local government, and how many were active in the last seven days.

## 12-gaps

**On screen:** slide up to Coverage gaps. Hold.

> Coverage gaps shows where you have no team on the ground. These are wards with no coordinator, and polling units with no canvasser. These are places you may want to ask your team to fill.

## 13-issues

**On screen:** slide up to Top issues. Tap **Persuadable**. Tap **All canvassed**.

> Finally, Top issues shows what voters say matters most to them. Tap, Persuadable, to see what matters to the people you can still win over. This is very useful when you are preparing what to say. Below that, you can see the top issues for each local government.

## Part 3: Before you visit a local government

## 14-open-brief

**On screen:** slide back to the top. Tap **Demo West** under Needs attention. Hold.

> Now, let us say you are about to visit a local government. Slide back to the top of the screen, and tap its name. This opens a short brief, to read before you go. At a glance, you can see how many voters are there, how many have been reached, and how many support you.

## 15-brief-details

**On screen:** hold on the rest of the brief.

> Next, you can see what voters there care about. Worth knowing, tells you about any problem in that area. And under, Team here, you will find the size of your team, with the name and phone number of your L.G.A. coordinator. So you can call them before you arrive.

## 16-ward-by-ward

**On screen:** tap **See ward by ward**.

> To look closer, tap, See ward by ward.

## Part 4: Looking closer at an area

## 17-areas-top

**On screen:** the Areas screen. Hold.

> You are now on the Areas screen. You can also open it at any time with the Areas button at the bottom. These four boxes show the voters on the list, the percentage canvassed, the percentage supporting you, and how many voters can be reached by phone.

## 18-where-to-focus

**On screen:** slide up to Where to focus. Hold.

> Where to focus, is your team's to-do list for this area. It shows which ward has the most voters not yet reached, which has the most persuadable voters, your strongest ward, and your weakest ward.

## 19-who-they-are

**On screen:** slide up through Gender, Age and Canvassing answers.

> Further down, you can see the voters in this area by gender and by age. Canvassing answers shows what people have told your team about their support, their PVC, and whether they plan to vote.

## 20-by-ward

**On screen:** slide up to By ward. Hold.

> At the bottom, each ward is listed with its own figures, so you can compare them side by side.

## 21-choose-ward

**On screen:** slide to the top. Tap the Ward box, choose **West Ward 2**.

> To see only one ward, slide back to the top, tap the Ward box, and choose a ward. All the figures on the screen change to show that ward alone.

## 22-choose-lga

**On screen:** tap the LGA box. Hold on the list. Choose **Demo Central**.

> To change the local government, tap the L.G.A. box, and choose another one from the list. You can search here too. Or choose, All L.G.A.s, at the top, to see your whole election area.

## Part 5: Good to know

## 23-updates

**On screen:** tap **Overview**. Slide up, and pull the screen down at the top to refresh.

> The figures update when you have internet. To get the very latest numbers, go to the top of the screen, and pull it down with your finger. If you have no network, the app will show you the last figures it saved, and tell you how old they are.

## 24-sign-out

**On screen:** tap the profile icon. Hold on the menu. Close it.

> Tap the profile icon at the top right to see your name, and to sign out. If you sign out, the figures saved on this phone will be removed, and you will need internet to sign in again. If you forget your password, please ask an Erims administrator to reset it.

## 25-closing

**On screen:** the Overview. Hold.

> Here is a simple routine. Each day, check, Needs attention, and Momentum. Before each visit, read the brief for that local government. And please keep these figures private, because they are your campaign's information. Thank you, and we wish you all the best.
