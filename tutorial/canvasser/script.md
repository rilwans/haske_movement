# Canvasser tutorial: how to canvass with the Haske app

Written for someone who has never used an app like this. Short sentences, one action at a time.
Each section is one clip with its own voice-over file. The narration never says the default password out loud.

**Demo details (local only, all made up)**

## Part 1: Getting started

## 01-welcome

**On screen:** the Sign in screen, nothing typed.

> Welcome, canvasser. This video will teach you, step by step, how to use the Haske app for your canvassing work. You do not need to be good with phones. Just follow along. You can pause the video at any time, and go back if you miss something.

## 02-what-is-canvassing

**On screen:** the Sign in screen.

> Canvassing means visiting the voters in your polling unit, talking with them, and recording what they tell you. The app gives you the list of voters in your polling unit. After you talk with a voter, you record the conversation on your phone. Your coordinators can then see how the work is going.

## 03-before-you-start

**On screen:** the Sign in screen.

> Before you go out, make sure of three things. One, your phone is charged. Two, you know your phone number and the password you were given. Three, you have internet for your first sign in. After that first time, you can do your work even where there is no network.

## 04-sign-in

**On screen:** tap Phone number, type `0800 555 0200`. Tap Password, type the default password. Close the keyboard. Tap **Sign in**.

> Let us sign in. Tap the box that says Phone number, and type your phone number. Now tap the box that says Password, and type the password you were given. Your password will show as small dots. That is normal. It keeps your password private. Now tap the green Sign in button. If the app says wrong phone number or password, check what you typed, and try again.

## 05-set-password

**On screen:** "Set your password". Type a new password in both boxes. Tap **Save password**.

> The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose your own password. Tap the first box, and type a new password. Use at least eight characters, and choose something you will remember. Tap the second box, and type the same password again. Then tap Save password. From now on, this is your password. Do not share it with anybody.

## 06-setup

**On screen:** "Setting up this phone". The location question appears. Tap **While using the app**. The voter list opens.

> The app now sets up your phone. It is downloading your voters, so that you can work without internet later. Please wait until it finishes. Your phone will then ask if the app can use your location. Tap, While using the app. This lets the app note where each conversation took place.

## Part 2: Knowing your screen

## 07-home-top

**On screen:** the voter list. Hold still.

> This is your home screen. At the very top, you can see the name of your polling unit. Under it are the polling unit code, your ward, and your local government. Please check that this is the right polling unit. If it is wrong, tell your coordinator.

## 08-sync-bar

**On screen:** the voter list. Hold on the green bar.

> Below that is a coloured bar. When it is green and says, All sent, it means everything you have recorded has safely reached your team. We will come back to this bar later.

## 09-voter-list

**On screen:** scroll the list slowly down, then back up.

> Further down is your list of voters. A grey circle with a letter means nobody has visited that person yet. A green circle with a tick means the person has been visited. Under each name, you can see their phone number, and the last four characters of their voter number, called the V.I.N. For people who have been visited, you can also see what they said last time. Slide your finger up and down the screen to move through the list.

## 10-search

**On screen:** tap the search box, type `aisha`. Tap the X. Close the keyboard.

> To find one person quickly, tap the search box near the top, and start typing their name. You can also search with their phone number or their V.I.N. The list shrinks to show only the people who match. To go back to the full list, tap the small X at the end of the search box.

## 11-where-to-start

**On screen:** tap **Not yet canvassed**. Then tap **All**.

> Under the search box is a row of buttons that sort your voters into groups. The number beside each button tells you how many people are in that group. When you start your work, tap, Not yet canvassed. These are the people nobody has visited yet. To see everybody again, tap, All. We will look at the other groups near the end of this video.

## 12-today-count

**On screen:** the list. Hold on "Today: 0 conversations".

> Just below these buttons, you can see how many conversations you have recorded today. It goes up each time you save one.

## Part 3: Recording a conversation

## 13-open-voter

**On screen:** tap **Aisha Yusuf**. Hold on her details.

> Now let us record a conversation. When you are with a voter, tap their name in the list. This page shows what we already know about them. Their phone number, WhatsApp number, V.I.N., gender, and year of birth. If you see the words, Not known, it means we do not have that detail yet. You can ask them for it.

## 14-open-form

**On screen:** tap **Record conversation**.

> Greet the person politely, and introduce yourself. Then tap, Record conversation, at the bottom of the screen. You will see a few simple questions. You do not have to answer all of them. Only record what the person really tells you.

## 15-support

**On screen:** hold on the support answers, then tap **Leaning support**.

> The first question is, do they support our candidate, Kollo Baba Jiya? There are five answers. Strong support, means they are sure they support him. Leaning support, means they like him, but they are not fully sure. Undecided, means they have not made up their mind. Leaning against, means they do not really like him, but could still change. Opposed, means they will not support him. Tap the answer that fits what they said. The answer you tapped turns green, with a tick. If you tap the wrong one, simply tap the correct one.

## 16-pvc

**On screen:** tap **Has PVC**.

> The next question is about the PVC. That is the Permanent Voter's Card, which a person needs in order to vote. Tap, Has PVC, if they have collected their card. Tap, Registered, no PVC yet, if they registered, but have not collected the card. Tap, Not registered, if they never registered. And tap, Don't know, if you could not find out.

## 17-plans-to-vote

**On screen:** tap **Yes**.

> The next question is, do they plan to vote? Tap, Yes, Not sure, or No, based on what they tell you.

## 18-issues

**On screen:** tap **Jobs**, then **Water**.

> Next, ask what matters most to them. This could be security, roads, jobs, education, health, water, electricity, agriculture, or the cost of living. Here, you can tap more than one. If you tap one by mistake, tap it again, and the tick will go away.

## 19-gender-age

**On screen:** slide the page up. Hold on Gender and age.

> Now slide your finger up the screen to see the rest of the form. Here you can see their gender and year of birth. If these are already filled in, just confirm that they are correct. If they are empty, you can ask the person, and fill them in.

## 20-contact

**On screen:** hold on Contact details.

> Next are their contact details. Phone number, WhatsApp number, and V.I.N. If a number has changed, tap the box, and correct it. The V.I.N. is printed on their PVC. If they do not want to share a detail, leave it empty, and respect their choice.

## 21-notes

**On screen:** tap Notes, type `Wants the borehole repaired`.

> Finally, there is a box for notes. Use it for anything else the person told you, that is worth remembering. For example, they may want a borehole repaired in their area. Tap the box, and type it in. Notes are optional.

## 22-save

**On screen:** close the keyboard. Tap **Save**. "Saved conversation" appears.

> If the keyboard is covering the Save button, tap the small arrow at the bottom of the keyboard to close it. Then tap the green Save button. Wait for a moment. You must give at least one answer or a note, or the app will remind you. Now the message at the bottom says, Saved conversation. Please save while you are still with the voter, so the right location is recorded.

## 23-after-save

**On screen:** the voter's page with the conversation card. Hold still, then tap the back arrow.

> The conversation now appears on this person's page. The small cloud at the top of the card shows whether it has been sent. A green cloud with a tick means it has been sent. If you visit this person again another day, tap, Record another. To go back to your list, tap the arrow at the top left.

## 24-back-on-list

**On screen:** the voter list. Hold on Aisha's green tick and "Today: 1 conversation".

> Back on your list, this person now has a green tick, with what they told you underneath. And your count for today has gone up to one. That is all it takes. Now you can move on to the next voter.

## Part 4: Special situations

## 25-no-network

**On screen:** airplane mode on. Search `fatima`, open her, tap **Record conversation**, tap **Undecided**, slide up, tap **Save**, tap back. Clear the search.

> What if there is no network where you are working? Do not worry. Carry on the same way. Find the voter, tap Record conversation, tap the answers, and tap Save. This voter has no year of birth on the list, so the app is reminding you to ask them. Everything you save stays safely on your phone.

## 26-offline-bar

**On screen:** the list, with the grey offline bar. Hold still.

> Look at the bar at the top. It is now grey, and it says Offline, with how many records are saved on your phone. Do not delete the app, and do not sign out while you have records waiting to be sent.

## 27-back-online

**On screen:** airplane mode off. The bar turns green. Tap **Sync now**.

> When your phone gets network again, the app sends your records by itself, and the bar turns green and says, All sent. If you want to send them straight away, tap, Sync now, at the end of the bar. If the bar ever turns red, tap the voter that shows a red mark, to see what went wrong, and tell your coordinator.

## 28-search-first

**On screen:** search `Peter Adamu`. "No voter matches". Close the keyboard.

> Sometimes, you will meet a voter who is not on your list. First, search for their name, to be sure they are not already there. If the app says, no voter matches, you can add them.

## 29-add-voter

**On screen:** tap **Add voter**. Tap Male, type year `1987`, phone `08005550299`. Close the keyboard. Tap **Add and record conversation**.

> Tap the green Add voter button at the bottom of the screen. The name you searched for is already filled in. Check the spelling. Tap their gender. Type their year of birth, and their phone number. Then, if they have their PVC with them, type in their V.I.N. This helps us avoid adding the same person twice. When you are done, tap, Add and record conversation.

## 30-new-voter-conversation

**On screen:** tap **Strong support**. Slide up. Tap **Save**. Tap back. Clear the search.

> The app takes you straight to the same questions you have already learned. Tap their answers, and tap Save. The new voter is now on your list.

## Part 5: Following up

## 31-follow-up-lists

**On screen:** tap **Persuadable**, then **Turn out**, then **Need a PVC**, then **Unsure of voting**, then **Visited**, sliding the row of buttons along.

> Remember those buttons under the search box? Slide them to the side to see them all. They fill up as voters are visited, and they tell you who to go back to. Persuadable, are people who are undecided, or leaning against. They are worth another conversation. Turn out, are supporters who have their PVC. Remind them to vote on election day. Need a PVC, are supporters who have not collected their card, or have not registered. Help them to get one. Unsure of voting, are supporters who said they are not sure they will vote. And Visited, is everyone who has been visited at least once, by you or by your team.

## 32-announcements

**On screen:** tap the megaphone. Hold. Tap back.

> At the top of your screen, there is a small megaphone. Tap it to read announcements from your coordinators. Please check it often. To go back, tap the arrow.

## 33-account

**On screen:** tap the profile icon. Hold on the menu. Tap outside the menu to close it.

> Beside the megaphone is the profile icon. Tap it, and choose, My account, to update your contact details or your bank details, or to change your password. You will also see, Sign out, here. You will not be able to sign out while you still have records waiting to be sent. If you forget your password, ask an Erims administrator to reset it.

## 34-closing

**On screen:** the voter list. Hold.

> Before we finish, here are a few reminders. Be polite and respectful to every voter. Only record what they truly tell you. Save each conversation where it took place, because your coordinators can see visits that were recorded far from the polling unit, without a location, or too quickly one after the other. Keep voters' details private. And at the end of each day, connect to the internet, and make sure the bar says, All sent. Thank you for your hard work.
